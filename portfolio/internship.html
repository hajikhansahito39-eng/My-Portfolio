My Internship Experience

Connecting academic knowledge with practical English language teaching.

My teaching internship at Decent Public Higher Secondary School, Makli, Thatta, was an important step in my development as a future English lecturer. During this experience, I had the opportunity to apply my academic knowledge in a school environment, prepare lesson plans, teach English lessons, manage classroom activities, and evaluate students’ work.

This experience helped me better understand the responsibilities of a teacher and the importance of effective communication, lesson preparation, student participation, and classroom management.

My Internship Reflection

My teaching internship at Decent Public Higher Secondary School was a valuable part of my development as an aspiring English lecturer. It helped me understand that teaching involves more than explaining subject content. Effective teaching also requires preparation, patience, clear communication, classroom organization, and awareness of students’ learning needs.

Preparing lesson plans helped me appreciate the importance of organizing ideas before entering the classroom. Teaching English lessons gave me an opportunity to practice explaining concepts and communicating with learners. Managing classroom activities showed me how organization and student participation contribute to a productive learning environment. Evaluating students’ work helped me recognize the importance of checking understanding and identifying areas that need further attention.

This experience encouraged me to continue improving my teaching skills and strengthening my knowledge of English language and teaching methodologies. I aim to use the lessons learned during my internship as a foundation for my future professional development and my long-term goal of becoming an English Lecturer.
